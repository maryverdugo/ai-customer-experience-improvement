# Customer Experience Improvement Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow turns the Customer Experience Analysis Framework into a repeatable process for analyzing customer feedback.

The workflow is designed to help a team move from individual customer comments to organized insights and practical improvement opportunities.

AI supports the analysis.

A human reviews the findings and makes the final decision.

---

# Workflow Overview

```text
CUSTOMER FEEDBACK
        ↓
COLLECT
        ↓
CLEAN & ORGANIZE
        ↓
ANALYZE INDIVIDUAL FEEDBACK
        ↓
IDENTIFY PATTERNS
        ↓
INVESTIGATE POSSIBLE CAUSES
        ↓
ASSESS CUSTOMER & BUSINESS IMPACT
        ↓
DEVELOP IMPROVEMENT OPTIONS
        ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
        ↓
IMPROVEMENT ACTION PLAN
        ↓
MEASURE & LEARN
